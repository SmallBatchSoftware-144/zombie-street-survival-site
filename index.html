Zombie Street Survival

Survive the endless night.

A side-scrolling zombie survival game for iPhone and iPad. Fight your way down the street, scavenge crates and houses, recruit survivors, and see how long you can last when the horde keeps coming.

Coming soon to the App Store Works fully offline No ads No in-app purchases No tracking

What you do

Fight to hold the street

Swing melee weapons, hold up a shield to block, or open fire with pistols, shotguns and a flamethrower. Each weapon feels different and every zombie type demands a different answer.

Scavenge and recruit

Search crates and houses for supplies and loot, then rescue survivors who fight beside you and cover your back.

Take the wheel

Jump in the car, repair it and run the horde down when the street gets too crowded to fight on foot.

Pick your survivor

Play as a roster of characters, each with their own strengths. Unlock new characters and weapons as you earn XP.

Endless, with goals

Rounds keep coming and keep getting harder. Chase achievements and watch your stats: kills per zombie type, best round, time survived and more.

Built for touch

Landscape play with large on-screen controls, adjustable volume, a colourblind-friendly mode, and a Fire / Block side swap.

Your privacy comes first

The game runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no network connection, so nothing about you is collected or shared. Read the full Privacy Policy.
