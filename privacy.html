Privacy Policy

Zombie Street Survival · Effective 11 October 2026

The short version: Zombie Street Survival does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It runs entirely on your device.

Who I am

Zombie Street Survival is made by Cameron Hasan (“I”, “me”), an individual developer. You can reach me at smallbacthsoftware144@gmail.com.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or any other personal information.
  • It does not contain advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not include third-party analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs.
  • It does not connect to the internet or send any data to me or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Game progress
Your unlocks, XP, settings (such as volume and control layout), statistics and achievements are saved in a protected file inside the app on your device. I cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the app or use the erase option in Settings.
Troubleshooting log
The game may keep a small text log of technical events (for example, a game error) on your device to help diagnose problems. It is not transmitted anywhere. It contains no personal information and is removed when you delete the app.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, notifications or any other protected data on your device.

Information from Apple

If you download the game from the App Store, Apple handles your purchase and your Apple account. Apple may also give developers anonymous, aggregated information such as sales figures, and crash reports if you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings. I cannot identify you from this information. Apple's use of your data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. It contains cartoon zombie violence, so please check the age rating on the App Store.

Your rights

Because I do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. If you are in a region with data protection rights (such as the UK or EU) and have any question about them, you are welcome to email me.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, by adding online features), I will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before that version is released. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
